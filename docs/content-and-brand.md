# Content and brand guide

## Identity

Use **Markey Tech Solutions** on first reference and **MTS** where space is limited. The established slogan is “You turn it off, we turn it back on.”

The visual direction discussed for the brand uses a clean, modern treatment with computer and networking elements. Keep small-format profile artwork readable and avoid placing detailed service lists in a logo.

## Service themes

- Computer troubleshooting, repairs, upgrades, and custom builds.
- Printer setup and everyday device configuration.
- Wi-Fi and network setup.
- Malware cleanup and operating-system installation.
- Data-recovery assistance, subject to the condition of the storage device.
- Server, business-software, and camera-system setup where supported.

These are project content themes, not a guarantee that every service is available at a particular price or date. The website and an agreed customer quote should carry current terms.

## Publishing standards

Explain the customer's problem and the service clearly. Use project-owned artwork. Keep customer names, repair records, private screenshots, and contact details out of example content. Do not imply vendor partnerships or certifications beyond those actually held.
